Spengler Advent

An installable phone app that gives Spengler Cup fans a reason to come back every morning: a daily challenge, predictions on the next games, and cards that unlock the story behind each performance.

The core idea

Visitors check the score and leave. Every feature is judged by one test: does it make someone want to open the app again tomorrow?

Pillar 1

Daily Challenges

Five questions a day, one per category: Hockey 101, Hidden Hero, Higher or Lower, Guess the Player and Tournament History. Every answer ends in a "why it mattered" reveal.

Pillar 2

Predictions

Stake Pucks on game winner, best player of the day, best power play. Tomorrow's results settle yesterday's bets. Play money only, never real money.

Pillar 3

Cards

Players, Moments, Goalies, Teams, Rules and concepts, Game of the Day, Achievements. Earned from the daily pack, quizzes and Pucks. Upgrade Normal, Silver, Gold, Diamond to unlock deeper explanation. Complete a set for a bonus.

Pillar 4 · version 2

Social

Connect with friends and exchange cards. Shown as a design slide for now; card ownership is modelled so trading can be added later.

Replay mode

The 2026 games haven't happened, so predictions can't settle on real results. The demo includes a time machine that replays the 2025 edition day by day (six game days plus a recap day), and the jury can advance a day to see yesterday's predictions pay out.

What the data holds

Event data with coordinates and xG exists for one game, the 2025 Final (Davos 6:3 U.S. Collegiate Selects), with a full box score for skaters and goalies. The other ten 2025 games have real results from the Event Report. Earlier editions (1923 to 2025) come from public sources and drive Tournament History and Team cards. So winner predictions settle on every game day, while best player and best power play exist only on the Final. Details: docs/data-notes.md.

Tasks

Each task is test first: write the failing test, watch it fail, implement, watch it pass, commit.

Review focus

Inputs nobody will think to test. Each has a named test in the task that owns it.

  • Replay past the last day stops gracefully, and a game's prediction never settles early. (Tasks 8, 12)
  • A bet with too few Pucks, after lock time, or twice on one market is rejected; settling twice pays once. (Task 12)
  • A results-only day still gets five questions, a winner market and a Game of the Day card, and never shows a player stat from a game not yet replayed. (Tasks 5, 7, 15)
  • Claiming a set bonus twice pays once, and an achievement awards its card once. (Task 11)
  • Reloading mid-challenge or corrupted storage never loses progress or double-awards. (Tasks 8, 14)

Risks and open questions

Data terms

Don't publish the organizers' files until their terms are confirmed. The public repo holds code and processed aggregates, never raw event rows, and credits Wikipedia for the history table.

One game of events

Only the 2025 Final has events and a box score. Earlier replay days use results, and their player questions use a labelled Final spotlight.

Report transcription

The 2025 results are typed from report images. Tests check that period scores sum to final scores and group tables match.

Content volume

At least 16 questions (8 Hockey 101, 8 History) and the rule cards are hand-written. Start on day one.

Assets

Cards are typographic, so no images are needed. Logo, mascot and historic-player cards wait for assets from the organizers.

Submission time

The cut-off hour on 14 Nov is unknown. Plan to submit in the morning.
