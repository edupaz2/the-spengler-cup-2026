# Demo video script (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

Record on a phone-sized browser window at the final build. Export MP4 or MOV and check the duration in the file's properties (under 3:00, otherwise disqualified).

| Time | Beat | On screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:20 | Problem | Title, then the results table | "1.3 million visits in five days, and most people leave after seconds. A table says what happened, not why it mattered, and gives no reason to return." |
| 0:20 to 0:30 | Idea | Gift deck on the home screen | "The Spengler Cup: every morning at 08:00 a new deck of cards is waiting." |
| 0:30 to 2:30 | The loop on a phone (2:00) | See below | Short, plain narration of each tap |
| 2:30 to 2:45 | The insight | Win-probability line on a Gold card back | "The data does the explaining: the turning point, where the shots came from, how the goalie played." |
| 2:45 to 2:55 | Next steps | End slide | "Next: more games' data, social trading, push reminders. It is a static site with no accounts and no real money, so it can launch as it is." |

## The loop (0:30 to 2:30)
1. Tear open today's gift deck, reveal three cards one by one, swipe the games carousel (0:15).
2. Play the five questions: one rules question with its reveal, Hidden Hero with the real names after, Guess the Player taking a second clue (0:55).
3. Predictions: label "play money", place a bet (0:15).
4. Advance the replay day: the settle sheet shows the win and payout (0:15).
5. Cards: open the Album, flip a card, upgrade to Silver, show a Gold back (0:20).

Rehearse twice; cut a question reveal if over time. Mention the replay: "The 2026 tournament has not happened yet, so we replay the 2025 edition."
